# Contributing to 4PILLARS Policy Commons

We welcome research, drafting, data work, design, policy critique, and local implementation knowledge. The standard is not ideological agreement. It is whether a contribution makes a proposal more accurate, more lawful, more fair, and easier for the public to inspect.

## Before opening a pull request

- Search existing issues and policy modules.
- Use primary sources where possible: statutes, agency data, official financial disclosures, court opinions, peer-reviewed research, or original datasets.
- Clearly distinguish a fact, an estimate, a recommendation, and an unresolved question.
- Explain foreseeable harms, implementation cost, and who may be excluded or burdened.
- Avoid personal attacks and unsupported accusations about individuals or organizations.

## Required policy-proposal fields

Use the proposal template. Every policy proposal needs:

1. a defined problem and jurisdiction;
2. a proposed legal or administrative mechanism;
3. evidence and competing evidence;
4. budget, staffing, privacy, civil-rights, and enforcement implications;
5. alternatives considered;
6. measurable success and failure conditions; and
7. disclosure of material conflicts by the author or sponsoring organization.

## Review labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `draft` | Early work; not a project position |
| `evidence-needed` | Material factual support is missing |
| `legal-review` | Jurisdiction-specific legal analysis required |
| `public-comment` | Open for structured community comment |
| `adopted-model` | Approved as a reusable model, not binding law |
| `archived` | Superseded, withdrawn, or not advanced |

## Sensitive boundaries

Do not use this repository to coordinate candidate spending, solicit restricted contributions, evade disclosure rules, target protected classes, distribute personal information, or create financial products. Separate regulated organizations must establish their own counsel-approved processes, accounts, staff controls, and records.
