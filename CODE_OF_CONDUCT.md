# Code of Conduct

4PILLARS exists to make public systems more inspectable and more accountable. We expect participants to challenge ideas hard while treating people with dignity.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination, knowingly false factual claims, impersonation, coordinated manipulation of votes or comments, and instructions to evade campaign-finance, lobbying, privacy, or securities laws.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or refer credible threats or unlawful activity to appropriate authorities. Report concerns privately through the project’s designated contact channel; do not post sensitive evidence in public issues.
