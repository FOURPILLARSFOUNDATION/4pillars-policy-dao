# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is 4PILLARS partisan?

No. The project takes positions on evidence, rights, enforceability, and public accountability—not on political party loyalty.

## Can I donate and get a return?

No. 4PILLARS does not offer an investment, token, equity interest, revenue share, or guaranteed outcome.

## Does a model policy become law when the DAO votes for it?

No. A vote adopts a project model. It still needs lawful action by the relevant public body, and may need substantial adaptation for each jurisdiction.

## Can the Integrity Engine accuse someone of corruption?

No. It should present traceable records, methods, uncertainty, and a correction process. An automated match is not a finding of wrongdoing.

## Can the project fund candidates or campaigns?

Not through the Commons. Any regulated political activity would require a separately governed, counsel-reviewed entity with its own compliance controls.
