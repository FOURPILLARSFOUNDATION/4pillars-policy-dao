# Governance Guide

The Commons is designed as a policy DAO in the literal sense: members can deliberate, make recorded decisions, and hold the process accountable. It is not a financial DAO.

## Core rules

- Membership is participation, not ownership.
- Governance power cannot be bought.
- No token is required and no contributor is promised a financial return.
- Major decisions need an evidence record, comment period, conflict disclosures, and a public result.
- Direct and substantial conflicts require recusal from final review or voting.
- Founders may serve as unpaid volunteers while the project has no funds. Future compensation requires a conflict-free governing-body decision made in advance; it is never automatic or retroactive.

## Who does what

| Group | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Members | Propose, review, comment, vote, and appeal. |
| Working groups | Research defined topics and recommend action. |
| Stewards | Maintain process, repository integrity, and conduct rules. |
| Separate regulated entities | Only if needed for charitable, lobbying, ballot, or political activities—and with their own counsel, records, and governance. |

Read the complete [DAO Constitution](../governance/DAO-CONSTITUTION.md) and use the [Decision Record Template](../governance/DECISION-RECORD-TEMPLATE.md) for consequential decisions.

## Founder volunteer service and future pay

The founding stage should be explicit: voluntary work does not create a promise of back pay, equity, token allocations, revenue share, or compensation from future donations or grants. Personal and project funds should be kept separate, and a founder cannot approve their own expense reimbursement.

Before any founder or insider is paid, an authorized, conflict-free board or committee should adopt a conflict policy, review a written role and realistic budget, use comparable-pay data, and record a prospective decision while the interested person is fully recused. An adviser can provide data but cannot substitute for the authorized decision-makers.

Read the full [Founder Volunteer & Future Compensation Policy](../governance/FOUNDER-VOLUNTEER-AND-COMPENSATION.md).
