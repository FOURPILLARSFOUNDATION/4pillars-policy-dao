# 4PILLARS Public Wiki

Welcome to the public reference guide for the 4PILLARS Policy Commons.

4PILLARS is an open-source civic policy project built around a simple premise: public problems cross systems, so public solutions must be designed, tested, and implemented across systems too.

## The four pillars

1. **Engineer** — analyze the whole system, not only the visible symptom.
2. **Expose** — make sources, incentives, and conflicts inspectable.
3. **Organize** — turn passive agreement into informed civic participation.
4. **Implement** — test models in the real world and publish what happens.

## Our public promise

We will label what is evidence, what is an assumption, and what remains unknown. We will not sell governance power, promise financial returns, or use a black-box score to declare a person corrupt. Policy models remain open for challenge, adaptation, and revision.
