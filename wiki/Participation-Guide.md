# Participation Guide

You do not need to be a policy professional to help. You do need to be willing to show your work and let other people test it.

## Useful ways to contribute

- Bring primary sources, public records, data, and local implementation knowledge.
- Identify a weak assumption or unintended effect in a proposal.
- Translate a policy document into clearer plain language.
- Share the experience of people affected by a rule or system.
- Help build civic tools, visualizations, datasets, or accessibility improvements.

## Submit a proposal

Use the [policy proposal issue form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/policy-proposal.yml) or adapt the [policy template](../policies/POLICY-TEMPLATE.md). State the jurisdiction, problem, mechanism, evidence, risks, alternatives, and any material conflict you have.

## Community standard

Challenge claims hard. Do not harass, dox, make unsupported allegations, or treat disagreement as evidence of bad faith. Read the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Contribution Guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) before participating.
