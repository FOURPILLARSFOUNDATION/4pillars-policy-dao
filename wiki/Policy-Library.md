# Policy Library

The policy library contains the project’s versioned work. A document’s status tells the public how far it has progressed.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `scoping` | The question and research plan are open; no project position exists. |
| `draft` | A proposed mechanism exists but review is incomplete. |
| `model` | A reviewed template that still needs jurisdiction-specific adaptation. |
| `retired` | Work that was superseded, withdrawn, or not advanced. |

## Current workstreams

## How to read a policy module

Look for the problem definition, jurisdiction, legal authority, evidence, alternatives, risk analysis, affected communities, enforcement plan, and measurable results. A persuasive headline alone is not enough to become a 4PILLARS model policy.
