# Retirement Resilience

**Status:** scoping  
**Question:** How can retirement financing share in productivity gains while protecting current and near-retirees during demographic and labor-market change?

## Baseline

Social Security’s financing and projected trust-fund status must be assessed using current official trustee reports, independent actuarial analysis, and clear assumptions. This project will not publish a depletion date as a certainty.

## Research tracks

- Revenue options, benefit adequacy, and distributional effects.
- Household impacts by age, income, disability status, race, gender, and caregiving history.
- Public investment or productivity-dividend concepts, including governance, risk, and constitutional authority.
- Transition rules that avoid abrupt benefit cuts or shifting risk onto retirees.

## Non-negotiable review questions

Who bears market risk? Who administers funds? What prevents political misuse? How are benefits protected in downturns? What happens if expected productivity gains do not materialize?
