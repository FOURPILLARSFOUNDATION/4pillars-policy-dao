How it moves

A policy cannot skip the hard parts.

  1. DefineFrame a specific problem, jurisdiction, and affected population.
  2. VerifyBuild an evidence dossier and show competing explanations.
  3. Stress-testReview legality, rights, cost, operations, privacy, and unintended effects.
  4. DeliberatePublish a versioned draft, gather comments, and document revisions.
  5. MeasureRelease results and amend the model when the evidence changes.

Rules before power

Participation is not for sale.

The Commons is designed as a policy DAO, not a speculative token project. Membership grants no financial return. Voting power cannot be bought. Important decisions require sources, public deliberation, conflict disclosures, and a durable record.
