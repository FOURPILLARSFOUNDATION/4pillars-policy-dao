# Public Support & Partnership Pack

This folder contains ready-to-adapt material for public crowdfunding, grant outreach, civic-technology partnerships, and responsible smart-contract exploration.

It is designed to raise support for an open public-interest project—not to sell an investment, token, voting power, or a promise of political influence.

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Crowdfunding Campaign](CROWDFUNDING-CAMPAIGN.md) | Full public campaign page and campaign structure |
| [Platform Posts](PLATFORM-POSTS.md) | Short-form launch copy and partner outreach text |
| [Smart Contract Brief](SMART-CONTRACT-BRIEF.md) | Deployment-free transparency and governance design brief |

Before any public solicitation, choose the correct entity and review platform terms, charitable-solicitation rules, tax treatment, lobbying/campaign-finance boundaries, and consumer-protection disclosures with qualified counsel.
