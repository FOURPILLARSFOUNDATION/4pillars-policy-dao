## Public launch post

**4PILLARS is live: an open-source policy commons built to turn evidence into implementable reform.**

We are starting with housing, automation, retirement resilience, and public integrity—because those systems do not operate in isolation, and neither should the solutions.

Every proposal is public, versioned, source-linked, and open to challenge. We are building the missing implementation layer: plain-language public education, model policy, accountable civic tools, and measurable pilot learning.
